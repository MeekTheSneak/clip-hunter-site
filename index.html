Clip Hunter
Privacy policy

Stream highlights, cut for Shorts

Clip Hunter is a personal tool. It finds the funniest moments in Twitch streams and turns them into short vertical clips for its owner's own channels.

How it works

  1. Finds the moment. It reads a stream's public chat replay and the clips viewers made, and picks the moments people reacted to most.
  2. Edits the clip. It cuts each moment into a 9:16 video with captions, a title and a credit linking to the streamer's channel.
  3. Waits for review. The owner watches every clip and rates it. Only clips the owner approves are posted.
  4. Posts to the owner's accounts. Approved clips go to the owner's own YouTube channel, Facebook Page and Instagram account.

How it uses YouTube, Facebook and Instagram

When its owner signs in, Clip Hunter uploads finished clips to that owner's own accounts and reads how those videos perform, so it can learn which clips work. It never acts on anyone else's account. The privacy policy has the details.

Who it is for

Clip Hunter is not a public service. There are no accounts to create and nothing to sign up for. It runs on its owner's own computer and is used only by them, for the Meek's Moments channel.
