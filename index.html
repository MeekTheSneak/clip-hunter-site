Clip Hunter

A personal tool that finds the funniest moments in Twitch streams and turns them into short vertical clips for its owner's own YouTube channel.

What it does

Clip Hunter runs on one person's own computer. It reads a stream's public chat replay and the clips viewers made, finds the moments people reacted to most, and cuts each one into a 9:16 video with captions. The owner reviews the clips and uploads the best ones to their own YouTube channel.

How it uses YouTube

When its owner signs in with Google, Clip Hunter uploads finished clips to that same channel and reads how those videos perform (views and watch time), so it can learn which kinds of clips work. It never acts on anyone else's channel. Details are in the privacy policy.

Who it is for

Clip Hunter is not a public service. There are no accounts to create and nothing to sign up for: it is used only by its owner, on their own computer.
